description: Never name the owner's other projects in this repository — not in rules, docs, code, commits or plans
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

Nothing committed or written in this repository names the owner's other projects (no "adapted from …",
"as in project …"). An idea borrowed from elsewhere is stated as this project's own rule, in its own words.

**Why:** nobody but the owner and me knows those projects; a reference to them is noise for every other reader of a
public repository.

**How to apply:** before writing a rule, doc, plan or commit, check it for names of other projects; in the
conversation they may be mentioned freely.
